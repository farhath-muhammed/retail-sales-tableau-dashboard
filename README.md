# Retail Sales & Profit Analysis | Tableau

An interactive Tableau dashboard that analyzes retail sales and profit across regions, categories, segments, and time, built to show where the business makes money and where it loses it.

🔗 **Live dashboard:** [View on Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/farhath.muhammed/viz/TABLEAUDASHBOARD_17918795026250/Dashboard1)

## Objective

Understand which regions, categories, and products drive sales and profit, and identify loss-making areas and the impact of discounts.

## Key Metrics (KPIs)

| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | 213M |
| Quantity Sold | 5,457 |
| Profit | 18.99M |
| Profit Ratio | 8.91% |

## Key Insights

- **South** is the top-selling region (57.8M).
- **Technology** is the leading category.
- **Phones** and **Laptops** drive the highest sub-category profit.
- Loss-making states are flagged using a calculated field, making problem areas easy to spot.

## What's Inside

- 23 worksheets built on a 1,000-row retail dataset
- Analysis of sales and profit by region, state, category, sub-category, segment, and ship mode
- Monthly sales trend analysis
- Top and bottom 10 customers and products
- Interactive filters for region, category, segment, and month

## Calculated Fields

- **Profit Ratio:** profit as a share of sales
- **Profit Status:** flags loss-making states and helps assess the impact of discounts on profit

## Tools Used

- Tableau (Desktop / Public)

## Dataset

1,000-row retail sales dataset. Source: ADD_SOURCE (for example, Kaggle link or "sample retail dataset").

## How to Use

1. Open the Tableau Public link above, or download the `.twbx` file from this repo.
2. Use the filters (region, category, segment, month) to explore the data.
